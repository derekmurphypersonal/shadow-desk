# Store page copy (itch.io / Steam)

**Title:** Shadow Desk
**Tagline:** The world is on your desk.
**Suggested price:** $3.99 (launch discount to $2.99)
**Genre tags:** Strategy, Simulation, Grand Strategy, Political, Turn-Based, Singleplayer, Historical, Modern

## Short description (under 300 characters)

Run the world from the Oval Office, one month at a time. Read your daily brief, weigh seven opinionated advisors, and decide where to send envoys, aid, spies or the Marines. Then watch the world answer.

## Long description

It's June 1990. The Berlin Wall is rubble, the Soviet Union is wobbling, and Iraqi armour is massing on the Kuwaiti border. Or it's November 2026, and the map is a tangle of wars, coups and quiet courtships by rival powers. Either way, the desk is yours.

Every month your cabinet briefs you. Your Secretary of State wants an envoy, Defense wants an arms sale, the CIA thinks a coup would work out just fine. Each crisis arrives as a page of the President's Daily Brief, read aloud by the advisor who wrote it. You decide. The world, about 170 countries with their own economies, armies, hunger and grudges, decides what happens next.

Win re-election, sign peace treaties, end famines, or bend the map toward Washington. Ignore your advisors and they resign. Get caught running covert ops and Congress starts drafting articles of impeachment.

**Features**

- Two eras: the end of the Cold War, and a multipolar 2026
- About 170 simulated countries, seeded with World Bank data
- Rival powers that court your allies with loans and arms
- Seven illustrated advisors with voiced briefings
- 83 events: the Gulf War, the Soviet collapse, Arctic standoffs, AI races, hurricanes and hostage crises
- A monthly newspaper, election-night returns and a final grade from history
- Four agendas, three difficulty levels, six endings
- Plays in about 2-4 hours per presidency; fully moddable JSON scenarios
